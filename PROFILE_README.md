## About Me

**Data Scientist & AI/ML Engineer** specializing in **production-grade Machine Learning**, **Generative AI**, **LLMs**, **RAG**, and **MLOps**. I build intelligent systems that transform complex data into measurable business, operational, and engineering impact across **renewable energy**, **healthcare**, **e-commerce**, and **enterprise analytics**.

AI creates value only when it solves real business problems. My work spans the full AI lifecycle — from **feature engineering** and **predictive modeling** to **cloud deployment**, **CI/CD pipelines**, and **Explainable AI (SHAP/LIME)** — delivering solutions that engineering and product teams can ship with confidence.

Currently at **Sriya.AI**, I develop AI-powered **Website Audit**, **Customer Intelligence**, and **Google Analytics 4** workflows using **Django**, **PostgreSQL**, **BigQuery**, **OpenAI**, and scalable ML pipelines. Previously at **Suzlon Energy**, I engineered **XGBoost** and **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)** for wind turbine fatigue forecasting at **73% accuracy**, with production **Docker** and **GitHub Actions** MLOps workflows.

**M.Sc. in Data Science and Analytics** from **EPITA University, Paris** · **B.E. in Information Technology** from **University of Mumbai**

**Open To:** Full-time **Data Scientist / AI/ML Engineer** roles · **Generative AI & LLM** engineering · **MLOps & production ML** · **Applied AI consulting** · **Remote & hybrid** opportunities · **Collaboration on open-source AI**
